How to fly

  • Engine left slider (drag up or down) · W/S · left stick
  • Tilt right pad · arrow keys or I J K L · right stick
  • Help big button · Space · A button. It flies the engine for you. You steer to the ring.
  • Touch down under 2 m/s down, under 1.2 m/s sideways, standing level.
  • Fuel counts in seconds of hovering. Chimes at 60 s and 30 s.
  • The Moon pulls 1.62 m/s², one sixth of the Earth, so a small push holds you up.
